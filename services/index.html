Services

Panel beating & spray painting services in Cape Town

Four main services, all done in our Elfindale workshop. Here is what each one includes.

Accident repair

Accident repair & panel beating

Collision damage repaired panel by panel in our Elfindale workshop, on all makes of vehicle.

After an accident you want the car to look and close the way it did before. We strip the damaged area, repair what can be saved, replace what can't, and check the gaps before any paint goes on.

  • Free quote from photos or a visit to the workshop
  • Damaged panels repaired or replaced
  • Bumpers, doors, wings, bonnets and boot lids
  • Panel gaps and shut lines checked before paint
  • Colour-matched respray in our own spray booth
  • Insurance and private work both welcome

Spray painting

Spray painting

Panel and full resprays in our own spray booth, from one bumper to a complete colour change.

Good paint is mostly preparation. We sand, prime and mask each car carefully, then spray inside the booth so dust stays out of the finish.

  • Single-panel and bumper resprays
  • Full resprays and colour changes
  • Colour matching to your existing paint
  • Proper masking of glass, trim and wheels
  • Sprayed in an enclosed booth
  • Polished before handover

Chassis straightening

Chassis straightening

Structural repair on a chassis straightening bench, for cars and bakkies that have taken a hard hit.

If the structure is out, new panels will never fit right. We mount the vehicle on the bench, anchor it and pull the damaged section back into line with a pulling tower and chains.

  • Vehicle mounted and anchored on the bench
  • Pulling tower and chains for controlled pulls
  • Front, rear and side structural damage
  • Cars, SUVs and bakkies
  • Panels fitted only once the structure is right
  • Finished in our own spray booth

Classic car restoration

Classic car restoration

Body restoration and paint for classic and sports cars, from bare shell to final polish.

Classics need patience. We strip the body, repair the metal, prime it and paint it in the booth, then build the car back up. Mercedes SLs and air-cooled Porsches are regulars here.

  • Body shells stripped and repaired
  • Rust and old repairs put right
  • Primer, paint and polish in-house
  • Shells worked on a body trolley for full access
  • Classic Mercedes-Benz and Porsche experience
  • Sports and collector cars welcome
