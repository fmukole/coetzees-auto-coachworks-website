Services

What we do

Everything from a scuffed bumper to a full body restoration, done in one workshop.

Accident repair & panel beating

From a bumper scrape to a heavy knock. We strip, repair or replace the damaged panels and line everything up properly before it goes to paint.

Request a quote

Spray painting

Panel resprays, full resprays and colour changes, done in our own spray booth. Each car is masked, prepped and painted under cover.

Request a quote

Chassis straightening

Hard hits can pull a car's structure out of line. We mount the car on our straightening bench and pull it back before any panels go on.

Request a quote

Classic car restoration

Bodywork and paint for classics. Shells stripped to bare metal, repaired, primed and painted. There's usually an old Mercedes or Porsche on our floor.

Request a quote

Dent & bumper repair

Parking knocks, dented doors and cracked or scuffed bumpers. Smaller jobs that make a big difference to how your car looks.

About us

Run by the Coetzee family

Coetzee's Auto Coachworks is a family panel beating and spray painting business with more than 20 years in the trade. For years we worked from Lester Road in Wynberg, and today you'll find us at our collision repair centre in Elfindale.

We repair accident damage on all makes, and we've built a name for bodywork on classic and sports cars. Whatever comes in, it gets the same care.
